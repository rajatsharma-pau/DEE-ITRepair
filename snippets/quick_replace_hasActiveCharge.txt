Quick fix if you do not want to replace full app.blade.php:

In resources/views/layouts/app.blade.php, do NOT use:
    $user->hasActiveCharge('Store Incharge')

Replace it with:
    $employee && method_exists($employee, 'hasActiveCharge') && $employee->hasActiveCharge('Store Incharge')

Reason:
    hasActiveCharge() belongs to App\Employee, not App\User.
